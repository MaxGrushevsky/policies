This policy covers the version with Google AdMob advertising for Android and iOS (version 1.3 and later). Earlier releases without advertising remain covered by the policy for the version without ads.

Local game data

No game account is required. Level progress, endless-mode records, theme, language and audio and vibration settings are stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server to receive these saved game records. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes local records; operating-system backup services may retain or restore them under their own settings and policies.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch one to continue after a failed attempt or to receive extra in-game energy. Google and the advertising partners identified in the consent interface may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address (including approximate location derived from it), ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, and crash, diagnostic and performance information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, SDK operation and improvement, security and fraud prevention.

The game does not request GPS or precise-location permission for advertising. An advertising provider can derive an approximate location from your IP address. Google may process data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention even when ads are non-personalized.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check your advertising privacy choices and display a consent form when available and required. You can accept, refuse or manage the available purposes and partners. Open Ad settings in the game's settings to revisit your advertising choices when a privacy form is available. Refusing personalized advertising does not require you to stop ordinary play; limited or non-personalized ads may be available subject to your choices and provider rules.

On iOS, access to the advertising identifier for tracking requires Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. The game does not request this permission, so the iOS advertising identifier is not made available to it or to advertising providers for cross-app tracking; ads on iOS rely on contextual signals and Apple's SKAdNetwork attribution. If a later version requests the permission, you may decline it and continue playing. Android provides advertising-identifier controls in system settings. Device controls and consent choices do not necessarily erase previously processed information.

Rewarded ads are voluntary. An in-game reward is granted only after the advertising SDK reports that the reward was earned. Opening an advertiser's website or installing its app is subject to that third party's privacy policy. If no ad can be shown while the device is online (for example, in a region without ads), the game may grant the energy reward without an ad.

Analytics

The game has no analytics of its own: it does not use Firebase or any other analytics service and does not send gameplay events to the developer. The data described above are processed only by the advertising SDK and its partners.

Providers, transfers and retention

Google and the advertising partners listed in the privacy interface process advertising data under their respective policies and applicable roles. Their services may process data in countries outside your country of residence. Their retention periods and transfer arrangements are described in their policies. We do not promise a single retention period for all advertising partners.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps and the partner information in the consent interface.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. Email and forwarding providers may process this correspondence. We retain it as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us for information we hold. For data held by Google or another advertising provider, you can also use that provider's privacy request tools. We cannot identify a local save or a provider's advertising record solely from a support email address.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. Usia, toko aplikasi, dan situs web

Game ini ditujukan untuk pemain berusia 13 tahun ke atas dan tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun. Kami tidak dengan sengaja meminta informasi pribadi dari anak di bawah 13 tahun. Jika Anda yakin seorang anak telah memberikan informasi, hubungi kami agar kami dapat meninjau permintaan tersebut. Kelayakan untuk menyetujui iklan yang dipersonalisasi dapat bergantung pada usia dan lokasi; batasan SDK yang berlaku harus dipatuhi.

Google Play, App Store, dan sistem operasi Anda memproses informasi sesuai kebijakannya sendiri. Dokumen ini dihosting di Vercel, yang penyedianya dapat memproses informasi teknis kunjungan sesuai dengan Kebijakan Privasi Vercel. Kami tidak menambahkan pelacak iklan atau analitik ke situs web kebijakan ini.
