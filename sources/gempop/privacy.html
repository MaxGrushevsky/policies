Privacy Policy

Gem Pop

Effective date: October 2, 2026.

Gem Pop (the "game") is a mobile game by Tenvar ("we").

Data the game itself collects. None. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, energy, language, sound and vibration settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. The game has no sign-up or accounts, and we do not sell or share your data.

Advertising. The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos and interstitial ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data. Data is transmitted encrypted. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where required (the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown on first launch. You can change your choice at any time: in-game Settings → Ad privacy. You can also turn off ad personalization in Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

Deleting data. All game data stays on your device — clear the app's data or uninstall the game to delete it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes. We may update this policy. The new version is published at this same link.
