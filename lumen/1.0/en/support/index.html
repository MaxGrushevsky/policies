Support

Lumen: Lights Out · Developer: Anonymous Play

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to help.blizzard269@dralias.com and include your device model and the app version.

How do I play?

Tap a tile to flip it and its neighbours by the level pattern. Turn every light out to clear the puzzle.

What do stars mean?

Three stars mean you cleared close to the ideal move count (par). Hints can lower the maximum stars for that clear.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Does the game need the internet?

No. The game works offline and has no ads or in-app purchases.
