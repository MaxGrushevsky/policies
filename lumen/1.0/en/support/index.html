Support

Light Puzzle: Lumen Logic · Developer: Anonymous Play

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to help.blizzard269@dralias.com and include your device model and the app version.

How do I play?

Tap a tile to flip it and its neighbours by the level pattern. Turn every light out to clear the puzzle.

What do stars mean?

Three stars mean you cleared the puzzle in the ideal number of moves (par) or fewer. Using a hint does not lower your stars.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Does the game need the internet?

No. The game works offline. Only the optional ads need an internet connection.

What is energy?

The first move on a new level costs 1 energy; restarting it or coming back to it is free, and winning the level gives the energy back. Energy refills by 1 every 4 minutes, up to 25, even while the game is closed. When it runs out, you can wait or watch an optional ad for +5 energy.

Are there ads?

Only if you choose to watch one: for +5 hints when you are out of hints, or for +5 energy. Ads never interrupt a puzzle. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options to change your ad choices. There are no in-app purchases.
