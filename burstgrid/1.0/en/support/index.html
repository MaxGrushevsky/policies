Support

Block Puzzle: Puzzle Pine · Developer: Anonymous Play

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to help.blizzard269@dralias.com and include your device model and the app version.

How do I play?

Drag a piece from the tray onto the 8×8 grid. Full rows and columns clear. If none of the remaining pieces fit, the run ends.

What are bonuses?

Hint, Undo and Shuffle help when you are stuck. Their stock is stored on your device.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Does the game need the internet?

No. The game works offline. Only the optional ads need an internet connection.

What is energy?

A new Adventure level or the day's Daily challenge costs 1 energy when you place the first piece; retrying the same level is free until you win it. Endless mode is always free. Energy refills by 1 every 4 minutes, up to 25, even while the game is closed. When it runs out, you can wait or watch an optional ad for +5 energy.

Are there ads?

Only if you choose to watch one: for more boosters when you run out (+3 hints, +3 undos or +2 shuffles), or for +5 energy. Ads never interrupt a game, and boosters never lower your stars. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options to change your ad choices. There are no in-app purchases.
